Sensor wiring & I²C scan

Use the GPIO numbers wired to your sensors. Connect by USB first, then scan. Keep the board still during calibration. Successful sensor initialization saves the pins on the board.

Connect an ESP32 to scan for sensors.

Classic ESP32: GPIO 0, 2, 4, 5, 12–19, 21–23, 25–27, 32, 33. Use pins available on your board; GPIO 0, 2, 5, 12 and 15 also affect boot. Expected sensor addresses: MPU6050 0x68 and VL53L0X 0x29. An address response alone does not identify a sensor.
